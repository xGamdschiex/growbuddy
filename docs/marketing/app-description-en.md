🌱 GrowBuddy — Your Digital Grow Journal

GrowBuddy helps hobby gardeners document their plants through the entire growth
cycle — from seed to harvest. Works offline, local data storage, optional cloud
sync.

✨ FEATURES

📊 GROW JOURNAL
• Manage multiple grows in parallel
• Daily check-ins with photo, EC, pH, VPD, temperature, humidity
• Growth phases (seedling, vegetation, flowering, drying, curing)
• Streak system for daily discipline

🧪 NUTRIENT CALCULATOR (11 nutrient lines)
• Athena Pro Line + Blended (mineral nutrients)
• BioBizz Organic (Light-Mix / Coco)
• CANNA Terra + Coco A+B
• Green House Powder Feeding (Hybrids, Short Flowering, Grow)
• Atami B'cuzz, Advanced Nutrients Sensi pH Perfect, Hesi Soil
• Auto-factor (linear D1 → D7), CalMag calculation, Cleanse ramp (Athena)
• Pro tips per phase and week (e.g. "PK 13-14 only Bloom W4-6")

📈 INSIGHTS
• Per-strain statistics (yield, duration, score)
• Phase distribution and trend analysis
• Total yield, check-in count, strain diversity

🌬️ SCIENTIFIC CALCULATIONS
• VPD (Vapor Pressure Deficit) live from temp + RH
• EC unit conversion (mS/cm, ppm500, ppm700)
• Phase-specific target ranges (Veg, Bloom)

💾 DATA OWNERSHIP
• 100% local storage (localStorage)
• Backup export as JSON file
• Optional: Cloud sync via Supabase (EU servers)
• No ads, no tracking, no third-party data sharing

🌍 COMMUNITY (optional)
• Mark your own grows as "Public"
• Feed with posts from other growers
• Like, follow, report system

⚙️ MISC
• Multi-language (German, English)
• Adaptive icons + Material Design
• Dark mode (default)
• Daily reminder notifications

🔒 PRIVACY
GrowBuddy stores your data primarily locally on your device. Cloud sync is
optional and requires an account (email or Google OAuth). No ads, no tracking,
no sales to third parties.

Privacy policy: https://growbuddy-app.vercel.app/privacy

cultivation advice. For cultivation in Germany: please check current legal

🌱 Made in Mainz · A hobby project by a passionate gardener.
